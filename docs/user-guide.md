# User Guide — WRM

## For Employees

### How to Request a Workspace

1. Visit the Workspace Reservation portal
2. Browse the available workspaces shown on the homepage
3. Click a workspace card to view full details, including location, type, and max occupancy
4. Click Request This Workspace
5. Fill in the following fields:
   - Start date/time
   - End date/time
   - Location
   - Purpose of visit
   - Additional comments (optional)
6. Click Submit

Your name is filled in automatically as the requester and cannot be
changed. If your end time is set earlier than your start time, you
will see an error and be asked to correct it before you can submit.

### What happens next

- Your request is routed to the Workplace Agents team for approval
- Once approved, you will receive an email confirming your
  reservation details and cancellation instructions
- When you arrive, an agent will check you in
- When you are finished, an agent will check you out, which frees
  the space for the next booking

### Cancelling a reservation

Reservations cannot currently be cancelled from the portal. Contact
the Workplace Agents team directly, and include your reservation
number from your confirmation email.

### No-shows

If you do not check in for a reservation, it may be marked as a
no-show. Please cancel in advance if you know you will not be using
your reservation.

### Need help?

Visit the Knowledge Base on the portal for a step by step guide on
how to request a workspace.

## For Workplace Agents

### How to process a reservation request

1. You will receive an approval request when an employee submits a
   reservation
2. Open the request from your Approvals list
3. Review the requested workspace, dates, and purpose of visit
4. Approve or reject the request

Approving creates the Reservation Tracker record and sends a
confirmation email to the requester. Rejecting sends the requester
an email and closes the request.

### How to check a guest in and out

1. Open the Reservation Tracker record for the reservation
2. When the guest arrives, click Check-In. This sets the reservation
   status to Checked-in and the workspace becomes unavailable to
   others
3. When the guest is finished, click Check-Out. This sets the
   reservation status to Checked-out and the workspace becomes
   available again

### How to handle a no-show

If a guest never checks in and the reservation's start time has
passed, the No-Show button becomes available on the record. Clicking
it marks the reservation as a no-show and closes it.

### How to flag a workspace for maintenance

1. Open a Checked-out reservation
2. Fill in the Maintenance issue field describing the problem
3. Save the record
4. Click Needs Maintenance

This creates a Workspace Maintenance record automatically, assigns
it to the Workspace Maintenance Team, and marks the workspace as
under maintenance so it cannot be booked until it is resolved. The
Maintenance issue field must contain a comment or the action will
be blocked.

### How to claim a reservation or maintenance ticket

If a record has no one assigned, click Claim to assign it to
yourself. This is available on both Reservation Tracker and
Workspace Maintenance records.

## For the Workspace Maintenance Team

### How to process a maintenance ticket

1. You will receive tickets assigned to your team when an agent
   flags a workspace for maintenance
2. Open the ticket and review the issue description
3. Fill in Work performed once the issue is resolved
4. Set Workspace status to either Available or Decommissioned,
   depending on the outcome
5. Set Ticket status to Closed

A ticket cannot be closed while Workspace status remains In
maintenance. Update the Workspace status field first, then close
the ticket.

## For Administrators

### How to manage workspace inventory

1. Navigate to the Workspace Options table
2. Create a new record for each reservable space, setting Workspace
   name, Workspace type, Location, Max occupancy, a description, and
   an image
3. Once created, Workspace name, Workspace type, Location, and Max
   occupancy become read only

These fields are locked after creation to protect the accuracy of
the inventory. If a correction is genuinely needed, it must be made
by an administrator with direct table access.

### How to use the dashboard

1. Log in to ServiceNow
2. Navigate to the WRM dashboard
3. View reports covering:
   - Workspace availability
   - Reservation status distribution
   - Bookings by workspace type and by individual workspace
   - Maintenance ticket status
4. All reports update automatically as records are processed. No
   manual input required.
